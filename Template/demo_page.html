About the Store

Welcome to Craftora, where every piece tells a story. Founded by Hiba Sk, our store is dedicated to the beauty of slow-made, handcrafted art. From intricate crochet textures to timeless beaded jewellery, we curate items that bring warmth and elegance to your everyday life.

Our mission is to support the heritage of handmade crafts while ensuring every customer feels a personal connection to the artistry behind their purchase.
